Research

The Exceedance Design Effect

Effective sample size for thresholds when data comes in clusters. The same 1,000 rows were worth about 217 independent observations at the median and about 621 at the 95th percentile.

SureStop

Stop bad training runs early, with a bound you set on how often a good one gets killed. On PyPI with an Optuna pruner.

Coverage inversion

An oracle for closed-market hours: you pick the coverage level, it returns the band that delivers it, with a receipt anyone can re-derive.

The minibatch design effect

In progress. How many independent examples a minibatch is worth when several come from the same document or author.
