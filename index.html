Research

Calibration is a property of outputs: when you say 85%, does 85% happen? i work on earning that property honestly, auditing that it holds, and acting on it.

The Exceedance Design Effect: effective sample size for thresholds under clustering

arXiv:2608.21262. Set a cutoff that 90% of a population falls below, estimated from a sample. When the observations come in groups — pupils in classrooms, sentences in articles, prefixes from one reasoning trace — the fraction below the cutoff varies more than independence implies, by a factor the paper proves and the usual design effect gets wrong. So a dataset has no single effective sample size: the same 1,000 rows carried about 217 independent observations’ worth at the median and about 621 at the 95th percentile. The number of rows belongs to the dataset. The effective sample size belongs to the analysis. Code and the neff estimator in exceedance-design-effect.

SureStop: stop bad training runs early, with a bound on how often you kill a good one

Open source, on PyPI as surestop with an Optuna pruner. Conformal risk control on the loss “good and killed”: you pick α, the rule keeps the false-kill rate at or below it. Measured on 400 real LoRA fine-tuning runs under a pre-registered protocol: the bound held on both splits and about half the evaluation compute was saved. The run also found that every kill fired at the 3rd of 50 evaluations, which is the part worth reading.

Coverage inversion: a calibration-transparent oracle for closed-market hours

Paper submitted to arXiv. The consumer picks the coverage level; the oracle returns the band that empirically delivers it, with a receipt anyone can re-derive from public data. 12 years of weekend and overnight gaps. Reference implementation in soothsayer.

The minibatch design effect

In progress. When a minibatch holds several examples from the same document or author, how many independent examples is it worth? Measured on the correlation between the examples’ gradients, not their inputs, and tracked as the run trains.

Selection on the score

In draft. When calibration-set membership depends on the score being calibrated, the correction weighted conformal applies to covariate shift doesn’t exist. What replaces it is a sharp interval.

Twin fidelity without memorisation

Fine-tuning a model to carry a person’s idiolect while it learns none of their private facts — measured with extraction canaries on one side and authorship embeddings on the other.

Certified abstention

Models represent internally that their evidence doesn’t cover a question, and answer anyway. Reading that signal so every answer is grounded or escalated, under an error budget you set instead of hope for.
